GGSIPU USAR Student Code of Conduct and Disciplinary Regulations
University School of Automation and Robotics (USAR), Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU), East Delhi Campus, Surajmal Vihar, Delhi 110032. All registered students are governed by the university statutes, ordinances (principally Ordinance 3 on maintenance of student discipline), and institutional regulations designed to preserve a safe, inclusive, and academically focused campus environment.

Mandatory University Identity Card Regulations
Every student must carry and visibly wear their official GGSIPU / USAR laminated identity card at all times while present on campus premises, including academic blocks, laboratories, workshops, the central library, cafeterias, and sports facilities. Campus security personnel and faculty members are authorized to inspect student ID cards at any entry point.

Consequences of Non-Possession or Loss of Student ID Card
Entry into academic lecture complexes, computing labs, and semester examination halls is strictly denied to students unable to produce a valid physical identity card. In the event of a lost ID card, the student must file a Non-Cognizable Report (NCR) with local police, submit an application to the USAR General Administration Branch, and obtain a duplicate card after paying the prescribed university processing fee.

Zero-Tolerance Policy Against Ragging (UGC Regulations 2009)
In strict compliance with UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions (2009) and Hon'ble Supreme Court directives, GGSIPU maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging in any form, whether on-campus, inside hostels, in transit, or outside the university premises.

Definition of Ragging under University Statutes
Ragging encompasses any conduct by any student or group of students, whether by words spoken or written or by an act, that involves teasing, treating, or handling a fresher or any other student with rudeness, creating a psychological fear, physical assault, extortion, forced chores, or asking any student to do any act which causes shame or embarrassment.

Mandatory Anti-Ragging Affidavit Submission
Every newly admitted student and their parent or guardian must sign and submit a mandatory online Anti-Ragging Undertaking/Affidavit at the time of admission and at the commencement of each academic session through the national anti-ragging portal (antiragging.in). Verification receipts must be submitted to the school administrative branch.

Administrative and Penal Sanctions for Ragging
Students found guilty of ragging face immediate disciplinary penalties including cancellation of admission, suspension from attending academic classes and academic privileges, withholding of scholarships and results, debarring from representing the institution in campus placement drives, and permanent rustication or expulsion. Serious cases are referred directly to law enforcement authorities for criminal prosecution.

Campus Anti-Ragging Committee and Flying Squad
USAR maintains an institutional Anti-Ragging Committee and an active Anti-Ragging Flying Squad comprising senior faculty members, hostel wardens, administrative officers, student representatives, and local administration officials. Surprise inspections are conducted across campus hostels, cafeterias, common rooms, and nearby transit points.

Anti-Ragging Helpline and Reporting Channels
Any student who experiences, witnesses, or suspects ragging can report the incident immediately to the USAR Anti-Ragging Helpline, email the Proctorial Board at proctor.usar@ipu.ac.in, or call the 24x7 National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline at 1800-180-5522. All complaints are treated with strict confidentiality.

Prohibition of Substance Abuse, Alcohol, and Smoking on Campus
The entire GGSIPU East Delhi Campus is a strictly designated Tobacco-Free, Alcohol-Free, and Substance-Free educational zone. Smoking, vaping, consumption or possession of alcohol, narcotics, or prohibited psychotropic substances is strictly forbidden. Any violation results in immediate police notification, heavy financial fines, and suspension from the university.

Prohibition of Weapons, Violence, and Physical Assault
Carrying weapons of any description (including firearms, sharp-edged instruments, knuckledusters, or explosive crackers) on campus is strictly banned. Engaging in physical fights, verbal altercations, intimidatory mob behavior, or threatening faculty, staff, or fellow students invites immediate police intervention and rustication.

Academic Integrity and Anti-Plagiarism Regulations
Academic honesty is strictly enforced across all engineering coursework, laboratory assignments, minor projects, and technical dissertations. Submitting plagiarized material, copying laboratory code, using ungrounded generative AI without authorization, or engaging in unfair means during examinations results in zero marks and disciplinary referral to the Examination Disciplinary Committee.

Unfair Means (UFM) in University Examinations
Possession of unauthorized study materials, written notes, mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, or electronic communication gadgets inside an examination room constitutes an Unfair Means (UFM) offense. A student caught under UFM faces cancellation of the paper or debarment from the entire semester examination series.

Campus Traffic, Parking, and Vehicle Safety Rules
Students driving two-wheelers inside the campus must wear standard ISI-certified protective helmets at all times. A maximum speed limit of 20 km/h is enforced on all campus roads. Vehicles must be parked exclusively in designated student parking bays; parking in faculty bays or obstructing emergency fire exits results in vehicle wheel-locking and penalty fines.

Protection of University Property and Lab Equipment
Students must treat all university infrastructure, computing hardware, laboratory equipment, robotic arms, furniture, and green building installations with care. Defacing walls, writing on benches, damaging computers, or vandalizing facilities attracts heavy restorative fines and disciplinary proceedings under the Public Property Damage Act.

Gender Sensitization, Equality, and POSH Regulations
USAR provides an equitable, inclusive, and gender-sensitized learning environment free from gender discrimination or harassment. The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), constituted under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, investigates complaints impartially with complete confidentiality.

Hostel Residence Code and Curfew Timings
Hostel residents must adhere strictly to entry/exit curfew timings announced by the Chief Warden. Male and female students are not permitted inside each other's designated residential wings. Overnight absence from hostels requires prior written permission from parents and approval from the resident warden.

Prohibition of Unauthorized Demonstrations and Political Gatherings
Organizing unauthorized protests, hunger strikes, demonstrations, rallies, or political gatherings that obstruct regular teaching, administrative functions, or campus movement is strictly prohibited without prior written sanction from the Proctor and Dean.

Social Media Conduct and Digital Communication Protocol
Students are expected to exercise responsibility and decorum on digital platforms. Publishing defamatory remarks, abusive comments, unverified rumors, or objectionable material against the university, faculty members, or peers on social media groups constitutes a violation of the student code of conduct.

Campus Cleanliness, Waste Segregation, and Eco-Campus Guidelines
As a modern Net Zero Energy campus, GGSIPU East Delhi Campus enforces strict eco-friendly protocols. Single-use plastics are strictly banned across campus premises. Students are required to utilize separate colored waste bins for biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste located throughout the academic blocks.

Disciplinary Jurisdiction of the Vice-Chancellor and Dean
Every student admitted to USAR GGSIPU East Delhi Campus remains subject to the disciplinary jurisdiction of the Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor, the Dean of USAR, and the School Proctorial Board. Decisions rendered by the competent university authority regarding student discipline and campus conduct are final and legally binding.

Hostel Discipline and Ragging Prevention Squad Inspections
Surprise joint inspections are conducted across boys and girls hostel wings by the Proctorial Board and student council representatives. Possession of electric heaters, unauthorized electrical immersion rods, or hosting non-resident guests overnight without prior written warden sanction incurs immediate room cancellation and disciplinary fines.
